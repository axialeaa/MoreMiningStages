This update is mainly a fix for an oversight in the recent port to 26.3. Destruction textures now require a counterpart for translucent textures (e.g. stained glass) when Improved Transparency is enabled in the video settings. Please read the modpage/README for updated documentation!

- The `textures` field has been renamed to `frames`.
- Changed the `mining_stages.json` codec to support split paths for order-independent transparency counterparts.
- The built-in **Double Destruction** resource pack is no longer marked as incompatible.
